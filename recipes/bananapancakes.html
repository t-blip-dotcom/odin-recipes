This is how to make Fluffy Banana Pancakes

Description

Whipping up a stack of fluffy banana pancakes is a delightful way to start your morning. This simple recipe uses overripe mashed bananas to add natural sweetness and incredible moisture directly into the batter. Whisking the wet ingredients into the flour, baking powder, and a dash of cinnamon creates a thick batter that rises beautifully on the griddle. Cooking them slowly over medium heat ensures a golden-brown crust with a light, cake-like interior. It is a comforting, family-favorite breakfast that tastes amazing with maple syrup or extra fruit.

Ingredients

  • 2 Large ripe bananas, mashed
  • 250g All-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons Baking powder
  • 250ml Milk
  • 1 Large egg
  • Ground cinnamon and sugar

Steps

  1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar, and a pinch of cinnamon together in a large bowl.
  2. Mash the bananas thoroughly in a separate bowl until smooth, then whisk in the egg and milk.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredient mixture.
  4. Stir the batter gently with a spatula just until combined, leaving a few small lumps.
  5. Preheat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and lightly grease it with oil or butter.
  6. Pour a small ladle of batter onto the skillet and cook until bubbles form on the surface.
  7. Flip the pancake carefully and cook the other side for 1 to 2 minutes until golden brown.
