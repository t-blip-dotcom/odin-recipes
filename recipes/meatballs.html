This is how to make Juicy Meatballs

Description

Crafting the perfect homemade meatball is an effortless way to elevate your next meal. This simple recipe combines seasoned minced meat with aromatic fresh coriander stems, crisp onions, and your favorite choice of spices, bound together with a touch of cooking oil for maximum moisture. Rolling the mixture with oiled hands ensures smooth, perfectly round portions that hold their shape. Baking them at a high temperature creates a beautifully browned exterior while keeping the inside tender and juicy. It is a quick, versatile protein option that pairs beautifully with pastas, subs, or standalone appetizers.

Ingredients

  • 1kg Minced meat
  • Coriander stems
  • Diced Onions
  • Salt
  • Cooking oil
  • Spices of Choice

Steps

  1. Add the minced meat, chopped coriander stems, onions, salt, choice spices, and cooking oil into a large bowl.
  2. Mix the ingredients thoroughly until they are evenly distributed.
  3. Coat your hands with a little oil, pick up a handful of the mixture, and roll it into a ball.
  4. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
  5. Place the rolled meatballs onto a baking tray lined with a baking sheet.
  6. Bake the meatballs uncovered for 20 minutes, or until the center is no longer pink.
  7. Flip the meatballs halfway through the cooking time to ensure even browning.
