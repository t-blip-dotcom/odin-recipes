This is how to make Fragrant Beef Pilau

Description

Cooking a traditional Swahili beef pilau is a wonderful way to bring rich, aromatic flavors to your dinner table. This classic one-pot recipe combines tender chunks of beef with long-grain basmati rice, deeply caramelized onions, and a signature blend of whole pilau spices. Sautéing the garlic, ginger, and spices together in oil unlocks their essential oils, creating a deeply flavorful broth that the rice absorbs entirely as it simmers. Sealing the pot tightly ensures every grain cooks up fluffy and separate while infusing the meat with warmth. It is a celebratory dish that pairs perfectly with a side of fresh kachumbari.

Ingredients

  • 1kg Beef, cubed
  • 500g Basmati rice
  • 3 Large onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tablespoons Pilau masala spice mix
  • 1 tablespoon Garlic ginger paste
  • Cooking oil

Steps

  1. Boil the cubed beef with garlic and salt in a pot until tender, then set aside the meat and broth.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a large pot and sauté the sliced onions until they turn a deep golden brown.
  3. Add the garlic ginger paste and the pilau masala spice mix, stirring quickly for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Stir in the boiled beef chunks and let them brown slightly with the onions and spices.
  5. Add the washed basmati rice to the pot and stir gently to coat the grains with the spiced oil.
  6. Pour in the reserved beef broth and bring the mixture to a boil over medium-high heat.
  7. Cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid, reduce the heat to low, and simmer for 20 minutes until the liquid is absorbed.
